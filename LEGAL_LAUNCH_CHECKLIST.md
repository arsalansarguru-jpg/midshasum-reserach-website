# Legal Launch Checklist

The Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Risk and Regulatory Disclosures pages are **drafts**. They are explicitly marked not for publication and are excluded from search indexing until completed. No legal review is claimed.

Before publication, obtain qualified counsel's review and confirm:

## Entity and jurisdiction

- [ ] Exact legal entity name that operates the website.
- [ ] Registration/incorporation number, if applicable.
- [ ] Registered and/or business address.
- [ ] Country and governing jurisdiction.
- [ ] Entity responsible for privacy/data-controller decisions.
- [ ] Approved general contact and privacy-request contact.

## Privacy and data processing

- [ ] Actual hosting provider and its request/security logging practices.
- [ ] Resend processing terms, location, retention and any transfer arrangements.
- [ ] Upstash region, retention/TTL behavior and access controls.
- [ ] Whether any analytics, cookies, monitoring or other third parties will be added.
- [ ] Applicable lawful bases, purposes, retention periods and rights processes.
- [ ] Data-subject request, incident response and deletion procedures.
- [ ] Final privacy notice reviewed for relevant jurisdictions.

## Terms and content

- [ ] Ownership/license of site content, logos, research and third-party material.
- [ ] Terms for contact enquiries and external links.
- [ ] Governing law, dispute provisions, effective date and limitations reviewed by counsel.
- [ ] Website claims and financial commentary approved for each intended audience/territory.

## Financial and regulatory disclosures

- [ ] Confirm whether any regulated activity is actually undertaken or planned.
- [ ] Verify any authorization, registration or restrictions with qualified professionals; do not imply a status that has not been verified.
- [ ] Review research, simulated performance, market-risk and investment-advice disclaimers.
- [ ] Confirm any jurisdiction-specific financial-promotion or audience restrictions.
- [ ] Retain explicit disclosure that Ira Dev is fictional, AI-generated and not an officer or employee.

## Publication gate

- [ ] Replace every bracketed `[DETAILS TO BE CONFIRMED]` item.
- [ ] Obtain documented legal approval.
- [ ] Remove the draft notice only after approval.
- [ ] Change legal-page robots metadata to indexable only after publication review.
- [ ] Rebuild and inspect all legal pages before launch.
